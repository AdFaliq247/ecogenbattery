ABOUT US

At MFF Green Technology, we believe energy storage should empower people and protect the planet — not pollute it, endanger lives, or become waste too soon.

We exist to solve the problems caused by conventional batteries: chemical leakage, fire risks, short lifespan, and long charging times.

Founded in 2020, MFF Green Technology is a fully Malaysian-owned green technology startup committed to reshaping how energy is stored and used. We harness cutting-edge materials and innovative manufacturing methods to develop energy storage solutions that are high-performance, eco-friendly, non-flammable, fast-charging, and long-lasting.

We don't just build batteries.
We build trust in technology that protects people, preserves nature, and powers progress.
