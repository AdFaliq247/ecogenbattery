Frequently Asked Question (FAQ)

1. What makes Eco-Gen Battery™ (Booster) different from normal lead-acid batteries?
Eco-Gen Battery™ (Booster) is made of non-chemical materials and the casing is made of biodegradable plastic.

2. How long does Eco-Gen Battery™ (Booster) last?
Eco-Gen Battery™ (Booster) can last up to 20,000 cycles (around 10 years of use).

3. Is Eco-Gen Battery™ (Booster) safe to use?
Yes. It has a built-in Battery Management System (BMS) that protects against overcharging, overheating, over-discharging and short circuit, making it safer than conventional batteries.

4. How does Eco-Gen Battery™ (Booster) perform in hot or cold weather?
It maintains stable in extreme conditions (from -20°C until 60°C).

5. What warranty does Eco-Gen Battery come with?
The Eco-Gen Battery™ (Booster) is covered against manufacturing defects, faulty workmanship, or internal electronic component failure for 12 months from the date of purchase or registration.
